✅ Video + Poster URL Guide
  • Dropbox: share link lao, ?dl=0 ko ?raw=1 karo
  • Google Drive: share link copy karo as-is
  • YouTube: youtube.com/watch?v=... ya youtu.be/...
  • Telegram: https://t.me/channelname/5 (public channel)
  • Direct: koi bhi .mp4 / .webm link
  • Poster: Dropbox share link paste karo — app automatically display URL bana dega
